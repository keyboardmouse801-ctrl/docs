Pre-orders let you place an order when our instant stock runs out. Your order is queued and delivered from our daily delivery capacity, in batches spread over the scheduled days.

## When an order becomes a pre-order

There is no separate pre-order screen — the checkout handles it automatically on **step 1 (Amount)**:

- **Your quantity exceeds current stock** — the part that fits in stock ships instantly, and the rest is scheduled. This is a **hybrid** order.
- **Stock is 0** — the banner reads "Out of stock right now. Every order is a pre-order, delivered from our daily capacity."
- **Pre-orders are closed** — occasionally the store pauses pre-orders entirely ("Pre-orders are closed right now"), in which case only in-stock amounts can be ordered.

![Checkout step 1 with an amount above stock — the delivery panel shows instant and scheduled batches](/images/checkout-preorder.png)

## How the delivery estimate works

As you change the amount, the **Delivery** panel in the right column updates live:

- **Instant** — "Today XM Instant": that portion is delivered immediately after payment.
- **Pre-order** — dated rows like "Sep 28 400M Pre-order": each day's batch, up to our daily capacity.

Each market ID row on step 2 is tagged **Instant**, **Pre-order**, or **Split**, so you know exactly which listings fill when.

## After placing a pre-order

1. Complete payment as normal — pre-orders are paid up front like any order.
2. Your order enters the delivery queue.
3. Track progress on the [order details page](/view-order) — it shows "XM of YM delivered" and the next scheduled batch.
4. You get an email when delivery completes.

Pre-orders show a **Pre-order** (or **Hybrid**) tag in your order history, and partially filled orders show the **Partially delivered** status.

## Pricing

Pre-orders use the same per-million price and pack discounts as instant orders — you keep the discount even though delivery is scheduled.
