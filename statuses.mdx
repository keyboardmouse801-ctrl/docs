Orders move through several statuses from placement to completion. This page explains what each one means.

## Statuses you will see

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Awaiting payment | Order created but not paid yet — held for 24 hours |
| Payment received | Payment confirmed, delivery is being set up |
| Processing | Order is being fulfilled |
| Partially delivered | Part of the order has been delivered (common for large pre-orders) |
| Delayed | Delivery has been delayed — the timeline shows the new date |
| Delivered | Delivery finished |
| Completed | Order fully complete |
| Failed | Order could not be completed |
| Cancelled | Order was cancelled (e.g. expired unpaid) |

---

## Normal progression

A successful instant order follows this path:

```
Order placed → Processing → Delivered
```

For instant orders this usually completes within seconds. Pre-orders and hybrid orders stay in **Processing** / **Partially delivered** until their scheduled batches finish.

---

## Status descriptions

### Awaiting payment

The order exists but no payment has arrived. It is held for **24 hours** — resume it from **Incomplete orders** on the Overview or by clicking it in Orders. After 24 hours it expires automatically.

### Payment received

Payment confirmed; the delivery pipeline is being prepared. Instant orders move on in seconds.

### Processing

Your order is being fulfilled. For instant orders this is brief; for pre-orders it lasts until your queued batches complete.

### Partially delivered

Part of your order has been delivered — typical for hybrid orders and pre-orders fulfilled in daily batches. The order page shows "XM of YM delivered" plus the next batch date.

### Delayed

Delivery has been delayed. The activity timeline shows the new expected date, and you get an email notification.

### Delivered / Completed

All Pokécoins have been delivered to your market listings. The order is done.

### Failed / Cancelled

The order could not be completed, or an unpaid order was cancelled/expired. Check the activity timeline for the reason and contact support on [Discord](https://discord.gg/TfKV8Xeh2r) if you need help — a failed payment never means lost money.

---

## Tracking timeline

Every order has an activity timeline on its details page with timestamped updates:

- **Order placed** — the order was created
- **Processing** — fulfilment started
- **Partially delivered** — shows "XM delivered (YM total)"
- **Delayed** — shows the new expected date
- **Delivered** — the order finished
- **Failed** — the order could not complete
