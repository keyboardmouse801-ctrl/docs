Radium Store (formerly Right Spot) is a dedicated platform for purchasing Pokécoins, the virtual currency used in the Poketwo Discord bot. Since 2023, we have served hundreds of customers worldwide, delivering billions of Pokécoins with a focus on speed, security, and reliability.

## What we do

We provide a straightforward way to purchase Pokécoins and have them delivered directly to your Poketwo account. Our automated delivery system ensures that most orders are fulfilled within seconds of payment confirmation.

## Why trainers choose Radium Store

**Speed**: Our delivery system processes orders automatically. Once your payment is confirmed, your Pokécoins are typically delivered within seconds through the Poketwo market system.

**Pricing**: We offer competitive rates per million Pokécoins, with additional pack discounts on larger purchases applied automatically at checkout. Our pricing is transparent with no hidden fees — the processing fee on every order is **Free**.

**Payment flexibility**: We accept PayPal (which also covers credit and debit cards), UPI for customers in India, and Litecoin (LTC) via NOWPayments. This ensures you can pay using your preferred method regardless of your location.

**Security**: Your payment information never passes through our servers. All transactions are processed through established payment providers like PayPal and Razorpay. We only store the information necessary to fulfill your order and provide customer support.

**Support**: Our support team responds to inquiries quickly on our Discord server — it is the only support channel, so you always reach a human who can look up your order.

## How delivery works

Radium Store uses the Poketwo market system to deliver your Pokécoins. When you place an order, you provide your market IDs — unique identifiers tied to listings from your Poketwo account. Once your payment is confirmed, our system automatically buys your listings, 50M Pokécoins per market ID.

We are committed to providing a trustworthy service for the Poketwo community. This means:

- Fast delivery with real-time order tracking
- Secure handling of all transactions

- Regular updates to improve the purchasing experience
