## Choosing a payment method

Consider these factors when selecting how to pay:

**Speed**: PayPal and card payments confirm instantly. UPI confirms within seconds. Litecoin needs blockchain confirmations — typically 5–15 minutes.

**Availability**: PayPal and cards work globally. UPI is for India. Litecoin works anywhere but requires a crypto wallet.

**Fees**: there are none on our side — every method shows **Processing fee: Free** at checkout. Your bank, PayPal, or wallet may apply their own conversion or network fees on their side.

## Payment security

All payment processing is handled by established, trusted providers:

- **PayPal and cards**: processed directly through PayPal
- **UPI**: processed through Razorpay
- **Crypto**: processed through NOWPayments

Your payment information is never stored on Radium Store servers. See [Payment security](/payment-security) for detailed information about how your financial data is protected.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, you can:

1. Try the same payment method again
2. Select a different payment method
3. Contact support on [Discord](https://discord.gg/TfKV8Xeh2r) if the issue persists

Common reasons for payment failure:

- Insufficient funds
- Card declined by issuer
- Payment timeout
- Network connectivity issues

Your order stays in **Awaiting payment** until it is paid or expires — unpaid orders are held for 24 hours. You will not be charged for failed payment attempts.
