The Overview is the first page you see when you log into your Radium Store dashboard. It provides a snapshot of your account activity, your recent orders, and quick access to placing a new order.

## Accessing the overview

After signing in with Discord you are taken straight to the Overview. You can get back to it at any time by clicking **Dashboard** in the side rail (desktop) or the bottom tab bar (mobile).

---

## Page layout

The Overview page greets you by name and is divided into several sections.

![Radium Store dashboard overview showing stats, recent orders, and the side navigation](/images/overview.png)

---

## Statistics cards

At the top of the Overview, four stat cards summarize your account:

- **Total spent** — your lifetime spend, with the number of paid orders underneath ("Across N paid orders").
- **Active** — orders currently being processed or awaiting payment.
- **Completed** — orders that have been fully delivered.
- **Pokécoins** — the total Pokécoins you have purchased, shown in millions (M).

---

## Incomplete orders

If you started checkout but never finished paying, those orders appear here with a **Resume** button that takes you back to the payment step.

<Note>
Unpaid orders are held for **24 hours** and then expire automatically. If your order expired, simply start a new one.
</Note>

## Recent orders

Below the stats you will find your five most recent orders, each showing:

- **Order number** — the unique reference (e.g. `RS-25001`); use it when contacting support.
- **Pokécoin amount** — how many Pokécoins the order is for.
- **Status pill** — the current state (Processing, Delivered, Awaiting payment, etc.).
- **Type tag** — Instant, Pre-order, or Hybrid.
- **Date** — when the order was placed.

Click any order to open its full details page, or click **View all** to open the complete [Orders](/orders) list.

---

## Navigation

On desktop, a side rail on the left links to every dashboard section:

- **Dashboard** — the Overview page
- **Orders** — your complete order history
- **Settings** — account details and preferences
- **Search** — opens the search palette (see below)

The header also has a **Place New Order** button, a notifications bell (your latest order updates), and your account menu with sign-out.

### Search palette

Click **Search** or press **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on Mac) to open the search palette. Type an order number, amount, or status to jump straight to an order, or to a dashboard page. Use ↑/↓ to move and Enter to open.

![Search palette matching orders and pages](/images/search.png)

### Mobile navigation

On mobile, the rail collapses into a bottom tab bar — Dashboard, Orders, Search, and Settings — and a floating **+** button starts a new order.

![Mobile dashboard with the bottom tab bar](/images/mobile-overview.png)
