Radium Store uses Discord for authentication. This means you do not need to create a separate username and password. Instead, you sign in with your existing Discord account, and you stay signed in until you log out.

## Creating your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the sign-in page">
    Click **Buy Now** on [radiumstore.org](https://radiumstore.org) — if you are not signed in, you land on the **Sign in to Radium Store** page. Click **Continue with Discord**.

    ![Radium Store sign-in page with the Continue with Discord button](/images/auth.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Discord">
    You are redirected to Discord's official authorization page, which shows what information Radium Store is requesting access to.

    Review the permissions and click **Authorize** to continue.

    ![Discord authorization screen asking to grant access to Radium Store](https://ik.imagekit.io/awnish90/auth.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    After authorization, you are redirected back to Radium Store. Your account is created and you land on your dashboard overview.

    ![Radium Store dashboard overview](/images/overview.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What information we receive

When you authorize Radium Store, Discord shares only basic profile information:

- Your Discord username
- Your Discord user ID
- Your email address (for order confirmations)
- Your profile picture

We do not receive access to your Discord messages, servers, friends list, or any private information.

<Note>
  Concerned about authorizing Discord? Read [Why we require Discord](/why-discord) for a detailed explanation of how OAuth works and why your account remains completely secure.
</Note>

## Logging in to an existing account

If you already have a Radium Store account, the login process is identical to signing up. Open the sign-in page, click **Continue with Discord**, authorize, and you will be taken to your dashboard with access to your order history and account settings.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discord authorization failed">
    If the authorization fails, ensure you are logged into the correct Discord account in your browser. Try logging out of Discord and back in, then attempt the authorization again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signed in with the wrong Discord account">
    If you have multiple Discord accounts and signed into the wrong one, sign out of Radium Store from **Settings → Sign out** (or the sign-out control in the dashboard navigation), then sign in again with the correct Discord account. Make sure you are logged into the same Discord account in your browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email not receiving confirmations">
    Ensure the email associated with your Discord account is correct and check your spam folder. You can update your Discord email in Discord's user settings, and it will be picked up on your next sign-in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
