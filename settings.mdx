The Settings page shows your account details, email preferences, security status, and a direct line to support.

## Accessing settings

Navigate to **Settings** in the side rail (or the bottom tab bar on mobile).

![Radium Store settings page](/images/settings.png)

---

## Profile information

The first card shows the details that come from your Discord account:

- **Username** — your Discord username, labelled "From Discord".
- **Email** — the email on your Discord account, used for order confirmations.
- **Discord ID** — your numeric Discord ID, with a copy button (useful when contacting support).
- **Member since** — when your Radium Store account was created.

<Note>
To update your username or email, change it in your Discord account settings. The update is picked up on your next sign-in.
</Note>

---

## Email preferences

Two toggles control what lands in your inbox:

- **Order updates** — emails about order progress and delivery.
- **Promotions & restocks** — special deals, discount announcements, and restock alerts.

Transactional email about purchases you have already made always arrives regardless — it is essential to your orders.

---

## Security

The security card shows:

- **Discord — Connected**: your sign-in uses Discord OAuth, so we never see your password.
- **Active session** — the session you are currently signed in with, plus a **Sign out** button. Signing out asks for confirmation ("Sign out of Radium Store?") and ends the session; your account and order history are untouched.

---

## Help

The help card links to **Contact Support**, which opens our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/TfKV8Xeh2r) — that is the only support channel. Have your order number ready for faster help.
