The Orders section provides a complete view of all your purchases — active orders awaiting delivery, unpaid orders you can still resume, and your full order history.

## Viewing your orders

Navigate to **Orders** in the side rail (or the bottom tab bar on mobile) to open your order list. Orders are shown newest first, eight per page, with pagination controls at the bottom.

![Orders page with status filter chips, search, and CSV export](/images/orders.png)

## Order information

Each order row displays:

**Order number** — a unique identifier formatted as `RS-` followed by a number. Use this reference when contacting support about a specific order.

**Pokécoin amount** — the quantity of Pokécoins in the order, in millions (M).

**Status pill** — the current state of the order (see [tracking statuses](/statuses) for details).

**Type tag** — Instant, Pre-order, or Hybrid (part instant, part scheduled).

**Total and date** — the amount paid and when the order was placed.

## Filtering and searching

The orders page includes:

**Status chips**: **All**, **Completed**, **Processing**, and **Cancelled**, each with a count. Click a chip to show only matching orders.

**Search**: type into "Search by ID, amount or status" to match an order by its number, Pokécoin amount, or status label.

<Note>
Filters combine — for example, the Processing chip plus a search for `100M` shows only in-progress 100M orders.
</Note>

## Order details

Click any order to open its details page, which shows the delivery progress, tracking timeline, market IDs, receipt, and support options. See the [View your order](/view-order) guide for a full walkthrough.

## Exporting order history

Click **Export** at the top of the orders list to download `order_history.csv`. The file includes the order number, status, amount, Pokécoins, date, and payment status for every order — handy for your own records.

## Order status

Each order displays a status pill showing the current stage — for example Awaiting payment, Processing, Delayed, or Delivered. For a complete explanation of every status, see [Tracking statuses](/statuses).
