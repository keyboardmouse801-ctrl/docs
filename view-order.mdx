The order details page shows everything about a single order — live delivery progress, the tracking timeline, market IDs, receipt, and support links.

## Accessing the order page

There are three ways to open it:

- **From the confirmation page** — after payment completes, the confirmation screen links to **Track order**.
- **From email** — the confirmation email includes a tracking link. These links carry an access key, so they work even on a device where you are not logged in.
- **From the dashboard** — open **Orders** and click any order, or use the search palette (Ctrl+K) to jump straight to it.

## Page layout

### Order header

The hero at the top shows:

- **Status pill** — the current state (Processing, Partially delivered, Delivered, Awaiting payment, etc.)
- **Type tag** — Instant, Pre-order, or Hybrid
- **Placed date** and a headline like "300M Pokécoins" with a delivery summary
- **Complete Payment** button if the order is still unpaid — it takes you back to the payment step
- **Copy market IDs** button once the order is paid

![Full order details page with market IDs, activity timeline, receipt, and details](/images/order-detail-full.png)

### Progress

For orders being delivered, a progress bar shows **"XM of YM delivered"**, plus **"n of m market IDs filled · 50M per ID"** and, for scheduled orders, the next batch date.

### Stat cards

- **Delivered** — how much has been sent so far
- **Remaining** — how much is still queued, with the final scheduled date
- **Paid** / **Amount due** — the payment total and method
- **Expires in** / **Last update** — time left to pay (unpaid orders) or when the order last changed

### Market IDs

One row per market ID in the order: the ID (with a copy button), its amount, whether it is the Instant, Pre-order, or Split side of the order, and its own status — e.g. "Delivered · Sep 26" or "Scheduled · Sep 29".

### Activity timeline

Every status change with a timestamp — order placed, each delivery batch ("50M delivered (300M total)"), delays, and completion. This is the same timeline described in [Tracking statuses](/statuses).

### Receipt

A receipt-style card: Pokécoins × price per million, pack discount, **Processing fee: Free**, total paid or due, payment method, and the payment ID with a copy button.

### Details and help

- **Details** — placed/updated timestamps, your Discord handle, and your email.
- **Help** — "Problem with this order?" links to our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/TfKV8Xeh2r); support can look the order up by its number.

---

## Real-time updates

The page refreshes its data automatically about every 5 seconds — status, progress, and the timeline update in place until the order reaches a final state. No reload needed.
